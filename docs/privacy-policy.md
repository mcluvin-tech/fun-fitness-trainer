# Privacy Policy — Fun Fitness Trainer

**Last updated:** June 1, 2026

Fun Fitness Trainer ("we", "the app") respects your privacy.

## Data we collect

- **Account**: Email or Apple ID identifier if you sign in.
- **Profile**: Optional trainer voice text, macro goals, phrase library.
- **Meals**: Photos you choose to analyze, macro estimates, meal notes. Stored on device; synced to our servers if you use an account.
- **Usage**: Anonymous crash and feature analytics if enabled.

## How we use data

- Provide macro estimates via AI vision (photos sent to our servers, then to OpenAI).
- Personalize trainer chat responses.
- Sync your data across devices when signed in.

## Third parties

- **Supabase** (hosting, authentication, database)
- **OpenAI** (food image analysis and trainer chat)
- **Apple** (Sign in with Apple, App Store payments)
- **RevenueCat** (subscription management, if you subscribe)

## Your rights

You may delete your account and cloud data in **Settings → Delete Account**. Local data can be removed by deleting the app.
