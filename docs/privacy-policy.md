# Privacy Policy — Fun Fitness Trainer

**Last updated:** October 9, 2026

Fun Fitness Trainer ("we", "the app") respects your privacy.

## Data we collect

- **Account**: Email or Apple ID identifier if you sign in.
- **Profile**: Optional trainer voice text, macro goals, phrase library.
- **Meals**: Photos you choose to analyze, macro estimates, meal notes. Stored on device; synced to our servers if you use an account.
- **Progress photos (optional)**: Body progress photos you choose to take. Stored only on this device; not uploaded.
- **Activity (optional)**: If you connect Apple Health, we read step count and active energy burned for today so Larry can show activity and notify you at your step goal. This does not increase your daily food calorie target. This data stays on device except when included as numbers in coach chat context you initiate.
- **Usage**: Anonymous crash and feature analytics if enabled.
- **Friend challenges (optional)**: If you send or accept a 7-day challenge, we store the link between the two accounts and their display names. Body weight, progress photos, calorie totals, and food logs are not shared for this feature.

## How we use data

- Estimate macros from food photos. When possible, barcodes and Nutrition Facts panels are read **on your device** (no photo upload). If on-device reading is not enough, the photo may be sent to our servers and then to OpenAI for analysis.
- Personalize trainer chat responses.
- Sync your data across devices when signed in.

## Third parties

- **Supabase** (hosting, authentication, database)
- **OpenAI** (food image analysis when on-device reading is insufficient; trainer chat)
- **Open Food Facts** (packaged-food nutrition lookup from barcodes / product search; community database)
- **Apple** (Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, Apple Health / HealthKit when you connect it, on-device Vision)
- **RevenueCat** (subscription management, if you subscribe)

## Your rights

You may delete your account and cloud data in **Settings → Delete Account**. Local data can be removed by deleting the app.
